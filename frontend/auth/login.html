About SADIMS

SADIMS (Smart Agro-Defence Intelligence & Monitoring System) is a comprehensive platform designed to modernize agricultural management.

Purpose: To empower farmers with AI-driven disease detection and real-time field monitoring to minimize crop loss and maximize yields.

Target Users: Farmers seeking precision agriculture tools and Administrators managing regional agricultural portfolios.

Academic Project: This system is developed as an academic project for demonstration purposes. AI predictions are simulated for research software modeling.

Quick Help Guide

1. Farmer Login
Registered farmers should enter their mobile number and password used during registration. New farmers must register first via the "Register here" link.

2. Admin Login
Administrators use their assigned mobile credentials. The system automatically detects the role and redirects to the Strategic Command Center.

3. First Steps After Login

  • Farmers: Navigate to "My Fields" to add a farm, then use "Scan Crop" for AI disease analysis.
  • Admins: Check the "Global Overview" for system-wide statistics and management logs.

Need further assistance? Please contact your regional agricultural supervisor.
